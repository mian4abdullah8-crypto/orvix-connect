Welcome to ORVIX Video.

Ek video-sharing platform ka early design prototype, jahan log videos discover kar sakein aur creators apna content share karne ki taraf agla qadam lein.

What works in this demo?

Video browsing, search, category filters, sample video playback, Shorts-style layout, local saved videos, local like toggles, demo comments aur local video preview.

What needs a backend?

Real login, shared video uploads, creator channels, global likes/comments, subscriptions, recommendations, live streaming, copyright handling aur monetization.

Agar tum local upload karte ho, file tumhare browser se bahar upload nahi hoti. Is demo mein apni file select karne se koi public video create nahi hota.
